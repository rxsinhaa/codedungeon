## 🌟 THE ADVENTURE
**Code Dungeon** transforms routine coding practice into a legendary quest. Whether you're a lone sorcerer or part of a powerful party, you can solve C++ challenges in a shared, real-time environment. 

> **Level up your skills, earn gold, and conquer the dungeon!**

### ✨ FEATURES OF THE REALM
* 📖 **The Adventurer's Handbook** – New to the realm? Consult the in-game guide to learn the ways of the digital dojo.
* 🤝 **Live Party Play** – Code together with friends in shared rooms. Every keystroke syncs across the party instantly.
* 🗝️ **Private & Public Realms** – Choose your path: secure a **Private Realm** for you and your friends, or brave the chaos of a **Random Realm**.
* 🎯 **AI-Powered Quests** – Encounter unique, fantasy-themed coding challenges generated by our resident **AI Quest Giver** (powered by Gemini).
* 🏰 **Retro RPG Aesthetic** – Immerse yourself in a world of pixel art, parchment scrolls, dungeon strata, and atmospheric soundscapes.
* ⚡ **Instant Combat Log** – Write, compile, and run C++ code instantly via a secure remote execution engine (Piston API).
* 💰 **Progression System** – Earn **Gold** and **XP** for every bug defeated. Unlock new dungeon levels (Apprentice -> Master -> Legendary) as you prove your worth.

## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY
1. **Step into the Light** – Visit the [Live Site](https://codedungeon.vercel.app/).
2. **Identify Yourself** – Enter your wizard name to begin your journey.
3. **Choose Your Path**:
    *   **New Adventure**: Create a fresh instance (Private) or join the public lobby (Random).
    *   **Join Realm**: Have a code? Enter the 4-digit Session ID to warp directly to your party.
4. **Summon a Quest** – Use the **Quest Board** to generate a challenge from the ether.
5. **Cast Your Spells** – Write your C++ solution in the parchment-styled editor. Collaboration is key!
6. **Claim Victory** – Run your code. If it passes, you earn loot and glory!

## 🛠️ THE SORCERY (TECH STACK)
* **Next.js 15 & React 19** – Providing a fast, seamless portal into the dungeon.
* **Firebase Realtime Database** – The magical thread that syncs code, chat, and player presence across the globe instantly.
* **OpenRouter / Gemini AI** – The brain behind our infinite quest generation, crafting lore-rich coding problems on the fly.
* **Piston API** – A secure, sandboxed environment where your C++ code comes to life.
* **Tailwind CSS & Radix UI** – Forging a beautiful, accessible, and responsive retro interface.
