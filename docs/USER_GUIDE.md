# 📖 Code Dungeon: The Adventurer's Guide

Welcome to the **Code Dungeon**, a collaborative RPG where code is your magic and bugs are your enemies.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Enter the Portal
Visit [Code Dungeon](https://codedungeon.vercel.app/).
*   **New Adventure**: Choose **Private Realm** (play with friends) or **Random Realm** (join strangers).
*   **Join Realm**: Have a code? Enter the **Session ID** to warp to your party.

### 2. Identify Yourself
Enter your **Wizard Name**. This is how you will be known in the annals of the dungeon.

---

## 🎮 The HUD (Heads-Up Display)

### 📜 The Spellbook (Editor)
*   **Left Panel**: Your collaborative code editor.
*   **Real-Time Sync**: Every keystroke is visible to your party instantly.
*   **Language**: C++ (The ancient tongue of systems).

### ⚔️ The Combat Log
*   **Right Panel**: Displays execution results, chat messages, and system events.
*   **Feedback**:
    *   🟢 **Success**: Your code compiled and ran successfully.
    *   🔴 **Fizzle**: Compilation or runtime error.
    *   🟣 **Quest**: AI Quest Giver updates.

### 📊 Vital Stats
*   **HP**: Your resilience. Failed code runs damage you!
*   **Mana**: Spend 10 Mana to cast a spell (run code). Regenerates over time.
*   **Gold**: Earned by completing quests. Used to summon new challenges.
*   **XP/Strata**: As you complete quests, you level up and unlock deeper dungeon strata (Apprentice → Master → Legendary).

---

## 🏰 Gameplay Loop

### 1. Summon a Quest
Open the **Quest Board** and pay 500 Gold to summon a challenge from the **AI Quest Giver** (powered by Gemini).
*   **Apprentice**: Simple logic puzzles.
*   **Master**: Algorithms and data structures.
*   **Legendary**: Brain-melting optimization problems.

### 2. Forge Your Spell
Collaborate with your party in the editor.
*   **Read the Brief**: The quest comments explain the objective.
*   **Write the logic**: Implement the required C++ function.

### 3. Cast & Conquer
Click the **Wand Icon** to run your code.
*   **Pass**: You earn Gold/XP. The quest is marked complete for the party.
*   **Fail**: The party takes damage. Debug and try again!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

*   **Communicate**: Use the in-game Party Chat to coordinate solutions.
*   **Manage Mana**: Don't spam the "Run" button! Read your code before casting.
*   **Private Realms**: Want to practice alone or with a specific friend? Select "Private Realm" on the landing page.
*   **In-Game Guide**: Click "How It Works" on the home screen for a visual tutorial.
